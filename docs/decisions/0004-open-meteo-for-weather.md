# 0004. Open-Meteo for the weather forecast

Status: Accepted.

Weather and sea conditions come from Open-Meteo because it is free, needs no account or key, and covers both land
forecasts and the marine data an ocean swim needs. Each request carries only the latitude and longitude of a place
you set in Settings, never anything else about you, and answers are cached for three hours. The cost is no tides, a sea
forecast of about eight days, and a dependency on a service with no guarantee for personal use.
